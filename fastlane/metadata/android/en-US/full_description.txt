Ondalune plays your library from <b>Navidrome</b>, <b>Jellyfin</b> or any <b>Subsonic/OpenSubsonic</b> server, plus the music folders you pick on your phone, through a hand-tuned audio engine, with a player that takes its colours and motion from whatever is playing.

<b>Your music, your sources</b>
<ul>
<li>Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic-Advanced, Ampache and other Subsonic servers, and Jellyfin</li>
<li>Your server's playlists, marked as the server's, next to the ones you make in the app</li>
<li>Music folders on this phone: pick each folder in Android's folder picker; only those folders are read, with no storage permission</li>
<li>Edit a song's title, artist, genre and year in the app, without touching your server or files</li>
</ul>

<b>Speed &amp; pitch engine</b>
<ul>
<li>Playback speed from 0.80× to 2.00×</li>
<li>Preserve key: time-stretch without changing pitch</li>
<li>Analog varispeed: pitch follows speed, like tape or vinyl</li>
<li>Independent tuning: up to ±12 semitones, with a one-tap 432 Hz shortcut</li>
</ul>

<b>Live studio reverb</b>
<ul>
<li>Real-time stereo reverb on the audio stream, from Small Booth to Cathedral Arena, plus Dreamscape Wash for slowed + reverb</li>
<li>Custom wet/dry, room size, decay and damping, and five-band EQ presets</li>
</ul>

<b>Seamless transitions:</b> gapless playback, an album-aware crossfade of up to 12 seconds, and ReplayGain (track or album) that never pushes a song into clipping.

<b>Your Mix:</b> a fresh mix every time you tap play, drawn from your whole library, favouring what you love without letting one artist take over.

<b>A player that follows the music</b>
<ul>
<li>The player grows out of the mini player, and covers and colours fade into the next song</li>
<li>Colours read from the real cover; five animated backgrounds</li>
<li>An honest quality line under the cover: the file's format, what actually plays, and what changes the sound</li>
<li>Six interface styles, separate fonts, artwork shapes, Material You, light, dark and AMOLED themes</li>
</ul>

<b>Everyday listening</b>
<ul>
<li>Time-synced lyrics that glide with the song, with tap-to-seek, also on big widgets</li>
<li>Offline downloads stored privately in the app, easy to find with the Downloaded filter</li>
<li>Smart shuffle, songs like the last ones when the queue ends, sleep timer and scrobbling</li>
<li>Lock-screen playback, media notification and resizable widgets</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design:</b> no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no account. Passwords are encrypted with Android's Keystore. Ondalune talks only to your servers and to Wikipedia for missing artist bios. Optional song analysis runs on the phone.
